If the Calendar app is installed in your workspace, every card with a due date
also appears there — as an all-day item on the day it is due, or at its time
when the due date has one, across all the boards you're a member of. Nothing
to set up; setting a due date on a card is enough. A card's start date is not
shown on the calendar yet; the [timeline view](help://boards:filtering-and-sorting)
is where a span is drawn.

## What you can do from the calendar

Selecting a card's item on the calendar opens the card itself. The item is
read-only on the grid — to change the due date, change it on the card (the
calendar updates immediately). Cards from archived boards and archived cards
don't appear.

## Sprint dates

On a board that plans in [sprints](help://boards:sprints), each dated sprint
also marks the calendar: an all-day **Sprint 3 · Product launch starts** item
on its first day and a matching **ends** item on its last. They are markers
rather than a bar across the whole fortnight, so a two-week sprint does not
sit over every day between. A planned sprint with no dates yet leaves nothing
on the calendar. Selecting one opens Boards.

## Hiding the feeds

Calendar's sidebar has a **Card due dates** checkbox and a separate **Sprint
dates** checkbox below the calendar list, so deadlines and sprint boundaries
can be shown or hidden independently. Uncheck one to clear those items from
the grid on this device; your cards and sprints are unaffected. See
[Items from other apps on your calendar](help://calendar:event-sources) for
how these feeds work in general.
