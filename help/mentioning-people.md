## Calling someone into a card

Type `@` in a card's description or a comment, then start typing a name. Pick
someone from the list and they are notified that you mentioned them, with a
link that opens the card.

The list offers the people on that board and nobody else, so a mention always
reaches someone who can actually open the card. If the person you want is not
there, add them to the board first — see
[Sharing boards](help://boards:sharing-boards).

You will not find yourself in the list. Mentioning yourself would only notify
you about something you just wrote.

## When someone is notified

A mention notifies once, when you add it:

- **In a comment** — when you post the comment.
- **In a description** — shortly after you type it. A description saves as you
  write, so there is no Send button to wait for.

Editing around a mention does not notify anyone again. You can reword the rest
of a description, fix a typo, or come back a day later, and the person you
mentioned is not pinged a second time. Adding a *new* mention notifies that
person, and only them.

Removing a mention does not un-notify anyone — the notification has already
been delivered.

There is one difference between the two worth knowing: adding a mention by
**editing an existing comment** does not notify. Post a new comment, or add the
mention to the card's description instead.

## Where mentions arrive

A mention shows up in the notification bell, and — if you have them switched
on — as a desktop or phone notification. Opening it takes you straight to the
card.

## What a mention looks like

A mention shows the person's name on a tinted background, so it reads as a
reference to a person rather than as words someone typed.

The name follows the person. If they change their display name, every mention
of them updates to match — you do not have to go back and edit anything.

If someone later leaves the board, their mention stays readable rather than
turning into a code. The text still makes sense months later.

## On a phone or tablet

The `@` picker works the same way in the mobile app: type `@`, keep typing to
narrow the list, and tap a name. The keyboard stays up while you type, so you
can filter without losing your place.

Mentions are one kind of card notification. For assignments, replies, watched
cards and due-date reminders — and how to turn any of them off — see
[Notifications and watching cards](help://boards:notifications).
