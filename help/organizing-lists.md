Lists are the columns of a board, and they usually map to the stages work moves
through — something like To do, Doing, Done. A board starts with a few and you
shape them to match how your team actually works.

## Adding a list

Hover the gap between two lists and a `+` appears on a short vertical bar. Click
it to drop a new list into exactly that gap. This is usually what you want: a
new stage almost always belongs next to the work it follows, not at the far end
of the board.

To add one at the end instead, use **Add list** past the last column, or press
`⇧N` from anywhere on the board.

Every list's `…` menu also carries **Add list left** and **Add list right**,
which is the way to add a list at the very front of the board, and the way to do
it from the keyboard.

The name field stays open after you press Enter, so you can type several lists
in a row without reaching for the mouse. Each one lands after the last. Press
Escape, or click away, when you are done.

## Renaming and reordering

Open a list's `…` menu and choose **Rename list**, then type the new name. To
move a list, drag it by its title, or use **Move left** and **Move right** in
the same menu — the menu is the keyboard and screen-reader path to what
dragging does with a pointer.

## Collapsing a list

A list you rarely look at can fold down to a narrow spine that still shows its
name and card count, which gets it out of the way without hiding it. Choose
**Collapse list** from the `…` menu, or double-click the list's title. Click the
spine to expand it again. Collapsing is per-person: it changes your view, not
your teammates'.

Cards can still be dropped onto a collapsed list.

## Setting what a list means

A list carries a status that tells the rest of the board what landing there
means — whether cards in it count as not started, in progress, or finished.
Choose **Status** in the `…` menu to set it. This drives how done-ness is
reported, so a board with a differently-named final column (Shipped, Live) still
reports correctly.

You can also cap how many cards a list should hold at once — see
[WIP limits and aging](help://boards:wip-limits-and-aging).

## Deleting a list

**Delete list** in the `…` menu removes it. If the list still holds cards, you
are asked to confirm first, and told how many cards go with it — deleting a list
also deletes its cards, along with their checklists, comments and attachments.
That cannot be undone, so archive cards you might want back before deleting the
list they sit in.

An empty list deletes immediately, since nothing is lost.
